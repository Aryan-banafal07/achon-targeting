Step 1

Define HCP Universe

The refreshed universe comprises 31,225 HCPs across five key specialist segments. Specialty is now identified across multiple sources rather than a single one, which materially improved how many high-value Achon specialists the universe captures.

~77% overlap. 24,073 HCPs carry over from the previous ~24k universe, and ~92% of them retain the same specialty group.
~23% net-new. 7,152 HCPs are new to the universe in the current refresh.
Komodo drove the growth. The Komodo source moved from ~11K to ~14K HCPs, adding three months of incremental data through May 2026.

Where the net-new HCPs came from

  • Komodo6,256
  • DHC827
  • Orthos49
  • Treaters18
  • Medical List2
  • Total net-new7,152
Mutually exclusive. Each net-new HCP is counted against a single source, so the five figures sum to the 7,152 total without double counting.

Specialty prioritization order

Specialties are identified across DHC, Komodo and Veeva primary and secondary fields, then resolved to the most elevated group, first match wins.

  1. Pediatric Endocrinologists
  2. Geneticists
  3. Pediatric Orthopedists
  4. ENTs - introduced this refresh
  5. Pediatrics
  6. Orthos
  7. NP / PA
  8. Endos
  9. Others
Patch logic. HCPs are further elevated into Ped Endos, Geneticists and Ped Orthos using VOD specialty information where the multi-source result landed on a weaker group.
Step 2

Initial HCP Tiering

Three independent deciles are computed, one per claims source, and the best of the three is kept, so an HCP gets credit for whichever source shows the strongest relative engagement. Decile then combines with KOL, treater and specialty signals to produce the initial tier.

How the decile is built

  • Veeva decileQ77.4 patients + claims
  • Komodo decileQ77.4 claims + patients
  • DHC decileQ77.4 Mx + patients
  • Final decileBest of the three

Each metric is normalised to a share of its column total, ranked descending, and mapped to a 1-10 band on cumulative share. HCPs with volume floor at decile 1; HCPs with none sit at 0.

Initial tier rules, first match wins

Tier 1 KOL/QED flagged or known treater or decile ≥ 5 and Ped Endo/Geneticist
Tier 2 Decile < 5 Ped Endo/Geneticist or decile ≥ 8
Tier 3 Decile ≤ 7 and generates Q77.4 claims or decile 5-7
Tier 4 Fallback, none of the above

No HCP generating a Q77.4 claim lands in the bottom tier, and no Ped Endo or Geneticist falls below Tier 2. Both QC gates confirmed on the refreshed build.

Step 3

Patient Attribution

Specialty alone doesn’t earn a field call. This step builds the patient cohort, then runs an eight-cycle cascade to pick a single primary HCP for every patient.

Patient cohort

Q77.4 arm
≥ 1 Q77.4 diagnosis on an office E&M visit, and ≥ 2 distinct service dates
Voxzogo arm

Age scope
2 to 18 years, the on-label pediatric population

  1. 1Tier 1/2 Ped Endo or Geneticist, qualifying visit
  2. 2Vox prescriber, gated on a qualifying visit
  3. 3Tier 1/2, other specialty
  4. 4Tier 3/4
  5. 5Tier 5
  6. 6Visited HCP outside the universe
  7. 7Vox-prescriber stop-gap · not visit-gated
  8. 8Incidence-treater stop-gap · last resort

A rendered 3-year Q77.4 visit is required for any attribution. A Voxzogo fill alone no longer qualifies. Ties within a cycle break on office E&M visits first, so a clinician who actually sees the patient in clinic outranks high-frequency home-health billing.

Incidence treater NPI. A new metric was created in the treatment-status layer so that patient attribution refreshes correctly, and it supplies the final stop-gap cycle above.

Step 5

Affiliations

Every target HCP is linked to a mastered healthcare organisation, so the target list can be read at the account level in Step 6.

How accounts resolve

Accounts resolve from a single mastered NPI-to-account table. The earlier name-matching crosswalk is retired, and placeholder values are converted to true nulls so an HCP with no genuine affiliation is excluded rather than grouped under a dash.

Affiliations are sourced from the Infi universe and the VOD crosswalk, with DHC hospital affiliation and Veeva primary affiliation layered on for coverage.

Closing the coverage gap

Among the target pool, a set of HCPs had no direct mastered HCO available through either the Infi universe or the VOD crosswalk. Fuzzy matching and manual checks recovered mastered HCOs for a further group of them.

Implication. Higher coverage of target HCPs going into the HCO tier refresh, which makes the account-level view in Step 6 more complete.
Step 6

Account Tiering

Affiliated HCPs and their patients roll up to the mastered account, which is then assigned a priority tier. The target universe is frozen first, so account numbers stay reproducible between runs.

Priority tiering method

Signal-bearing accounts are clustered on log-scaled opportunity and quality features, then ranked by mean signal. Accounts with no signal at all skip clustering entirely.

Deterministic patient-volume floors then run in order, and the result is frozen so future rollups restore the locked tier rather than re-clustering and drifting.

What drives a tier change

Between refreshes, accounts move tier on real changes in the number of affiliated target HCPs, the count of unique and treated patients, and Q77.4 claim volume. Clustering results are locked after each intentional re-tier so routine rebuilds do not shift them.

P1 - EliteTop cluster among signal-bearing accounts, or promoted by the elite patient-volume floor

Step 7

Workload Index

The last step converts target tier and final decile into a single number that sizes each HCP’s fair share of the field’s fixed call capacity.

workload_index  =  32,000  ×  [  ( calls ÷ target_calls ) × 70%   +   ( final_decile ÷ target_deciles ) × 30%  ]

Calls by tier

  • Tier 118 calls
  • Tier 216 calls
  • Tier 312 calls
  • Tier 4 & 5excluded
Assumption. These per-tier call counts are a ProcDNA assumption for sizing the plan, not a value derived from the data. They can be re-set with the field team.

Geography

Each HCP resolves to one ZIP through a source waterfall, then maps to a state and territory via the ZIP-to-territory reference table, so workload can be balanced and compared by geography.

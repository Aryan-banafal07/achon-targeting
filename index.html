Step 1

Define HCP Universe

The refreshed universe comprises 31,225 HCPs across nine specialty groups. Five are the key Achon specialist segments (Ped Endos, Geneticists, Ped Orthos, ENTs and Pediatrics); the remaining four (Orthos, NP/PA, Endos and Others) make up the lower-value tail. Specialty is now identified across multiple sources rather than a single one, which materially improved how many high-value Achon specialists the universe captures.

~77% overlap. 24,073 HCPs carry over from the previous ~24k universe, and ~92% of them retain the same specialty group.
~23% net-new. 7,152 HCPs are new to the universe in the current refresh.

Step 2

Initial Tiering

Three independent deciles are computed, one per claims source, each built from that source’s Q77.4 patient and claim volume. The max of the three is kept, so an HCP gets credit for whichever source shows the strongest Q77.4 activity. Decile then combines with KOL, treater and specialty signals to produce the initial tier.

How the decile is built

  • Veeva decileQ77.4 patients + claims
  • Komodo decileQ77.4 patients + claims
  • DHC decileQ77.4 patients + claims
  • Final decileMax of the three

Each metric is normalised to a share of its column total, ranked descending, and mapped to a 1-10 band on cumulative share. HCPs with volume floor at decile 1; HCPs with none sit at 0.

Initial tier rules

Tier 1 KOL or Treater or decile ≥ 5 and Ped Endo/Geneticist
Tier 2 Decile < 5 Ped Endo/Geneticist or decile ≥ 8
Tier 3 Decile ≤ 7 and generates Q77.4 claims or decile 5-7
Tier 4 Fallback, none of the above

No HCP generating a Q77.4 claim lands in the bottom tier, and no Ped Endo or Geneticist falls below Tier 2. Both QC gates confirmed on the refreshed build.

Step 3

Patient Attribution

This step builds the patient cohort, then runs an eight-cycle cascade to pick a single primary HCP for every patient.

Patient cohort

Q77.4 arm
≥ 1 Q77.4 diagnosis on an office E&M visit and ≥ 2 distinct Q77.4 service dates overall. The second date may carry any procedure code.
Voxzogo arm
Any pharmacy claim where brand = VOXZOGO or generic = VOSORITIDE
Age scope
Patient age (2026 minus year of birth) of 2 to 18 years, the on-label pediatric population
Treatment
Never Treated no Voxzogo fill of any kind · Active last fill on or after (max fill date minus 3 months) · Discontinued last fill older than that cutoff

Attribution cascade, lowest cycle wins

  1. 1Tier 1/2 Pediatric Endocrinologist or Geneticist rendered a qualifying visit
  2. 2Patient’s Vox prescriber, gated on ≥ 1 qualifying visit by any HCP · a Vox fill alone is not a visit
  3. 3Tier 1/2, other specialty, qualifying visit
  4. 4Tier 3/4, qualifying visit
  5. 5Tier 5, qualifying visit
  6. 6Visited HCP not in the universe at all · Non-Target Attributed
  7. 7Vox-prescriber stop-gap, not visit-gated · fires only if cycles 1 to 6 are empty
  8. 8Incidence-treater stop-gap, not visit-gated · fires only if cycles 1 to 7 are empty

A rendered 3-year Q77.4 visit is required for any attribution, and a Voxzogo fill alone no longer qualifies. Ties within a cycle break in order on most office E&M visits, then most clinical visits, then most recent visit, then NPI. Home-health and private-duty per-diem codes are excluded from the visit counts, so daily home-health billing cannot out-rank a clinician who actually sees the patient in clinic.

Incidence treater NPI. A new metric was created in the treatment-status layer so that patient attribution refreshes correctly, and it supplies the final stop-gap cycle above.

Step 5

HCP - Mastered HCO Affiliation

Target HCPs are linked to a mastered healthcare organisation, so the target list can be read at the HCO level in Step 6.

How affiliations resolve

Affiliations are sourced from the Infi universe and the VOD crosswalk, with DHC hospital affiliation and Veeva primary affiliation layered on for coverage.

Affiliations were matched against the crosswalk table, by direct or fuzzy match, and the corresponding mastered HCOs were pulled.

Output: NPI to mastered HCO

An HCP with no mastered HCO is assigned null rather than grouped under a placeholder HCO. The result is the NPI-to-mastered-HCO table.

Implication. Higher coverage of target HCPs going into the HCO tier refresh, which makes the HCO-level view in Step 6 more complete.
Step 6

HCO Roll-up and Tiering

Affiliated HCPs and their patients roll up to the mastered HCO, which is then assigned a priority tier. The target universe is frozen first, so HCO numbers stay reproducible between runs.

Priority tiering method

Signal-bearing HCOs are log-scaled, standardised, then clustered with k-means (k = 3, 25 initialisations, fixed seed) on opportunity and quality features and ranked into P1/P2/P3 by mean signal. HCOs failing all four signal checks skip clustering and go straight to P4-Minimal.

Three deterministic rules then run in order at freeze time: demote P2 to P3 if unique < 6 and treated < 1; promote P3/P4 to P2 if unique ≥ 6; promote any non-P1 to P1 if unique ≥ 20. The result is frozen so future rollups restore the locked tier rather than re-clustering and drifting.

What drives a tier change

Between refreshes, HCOs move tier on real changes in the number of affiliated target HCPs, the count of unique and treated patients, and Q77.4 claim volume. Clustering results are locked after each intentional re-tier so routine rebuilds do not shift them.

HCO priority tiers

HCO priority tierDefinition
P1 - EliteTop k-means cluster among signal-bearing HCOs (highest mean standardised opportunity + quality signal), or any HCO promoted by the ≥ 20 unique-patient elite floor
P2 - StrongMiddle cluster, retained only if ≥ 3 unique patients or ≥ 1 treated patient; demoted back to P3 at freeze time if it fails the stricter floor of ≥ 6 unique or ≥ 1 treated. Any P3/P4 with ≥ 6 unique patients is promoted up to P2
P3 - TailRemaining signal-bearing HCOs: the lowest cluster, or a P2 candidate that failed its retention floor
P4 - MinimalNo signal at all: fails all four of target HCPs affiliated ≥ 1, unique patients ≥ 1, Tier 1/2 HCPs affiliated ≥ 1, ungated Q77.4 claims ≥ 3
Step 7

Workload Index

The last step converts final tier and final decile into a single number that sizes each target HCP’s share of the field’s fixed call capacity.

workload_index = 32,000 × [ (target_call ÷ SUM(target_calls)) × 70% + (final_decile ÷ SUM(target_final_deciles)) × 30% ]

Calls by tier

  • Tier 118 calls
  • Tier 216 calls
  • Tier 312 calls
  • Tier 4 & 5excluded
Assumption. These per-tier call counts are an assumption for sizing the plan, not a value derived from the data.

Weighting & capacity

  • Total call pool32,000
  • 70% Call sharetarget_call ÷ SUM(target_calls)
  • 30% Final decile sharefinal_decile ÷ SUM(target_final_deciles)
  • Eligible poolTiers 1-3 only

Geography

Each HCP is mapped to one ZIP through VOD address, then maps to a territory via the ZIP-to-territory reference table, so workload can be compared by geography.
